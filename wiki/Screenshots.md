## Editor

Your original is never changed. Crops are kept in Immich and can be undone; other edits save as a new photo.

### Crop and straighten

Free or fixed aspect ratios, rotate and flip, and a wheel to straighten the horizon.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicx17/mimick/main/docs/screenshots/editor-crop.png" width="360" alt="Crop and straighten" />

### Adjust

Exposure, brightness, contrast, highlights, shadows, saturation, warmth, tint, vignette, and sharpen.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicx17/mimick/main/docs/screenshots/editor-adjust.png" width="360" alt="Adjust" />

### Filters

Vivid, Warm, Cool, Dramatic, Fade, Vintage, Mono, Noir, and Sepia, previewed on your photo.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicx17/mimick/main/docs/screenshots/editor-filters.png" width="360" alt="Filters" />

### Redact

Hide faces, plates, or text with pixelate, blur, frosted glass, or a solid bar.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicx17/mimick/main/docs/screenshots/editor-redact.png" width="360" alt="Redact" />

### Save options

Keep the original's details on the copy, stack the copy with the original, or reset everything.

![Save options](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicx17/mimick/main/docs/screenshots/editor-save-options.png)
