Library

Browse everything on your Immich server from a native GTK4 window.

Viewer

Open a photo or video to see it full size, read its details, and act on it.

Editor

Your original is never changed. Crops are kept in Immich and can be undone; other edits save as a new photo.

Search

Smart search, the text inside photos, and filters for everything else.

Albums & sharing

Albums can follow a folder on your computer, and you can share them with people or with a link.

People

Name the faces Immich found and keep the people list tidy.

Map

See where your photos were taken, and pick the map style you like.

On a phone

Below 600 pixels wide a bottom bar replaces the sidebar, and every screen works down to 360 pixels with touch.

Settings & sync

Watch folders upload in the background, and the settings window shows how it's going.

On the desktop

Mimick keeps running in the background, with a tray icon and notifications.
