Unofficial Immich Desktop Client for Linux

Mimick

Sync local folders to Immich, browse your library, manage albums, and search assets — from a native GTK4 desktop app.

Mimick is an unofficial Immich desktop client for Linux. It backs up your photo and video folders to a self-hosted Immich server in the background, and its optional library browser lets you view, search, and manage your Immich photos from the desktop.

Install

Install from Flathub

flatpak install flathub dev.nicx.mimick

Note: Ensure you have the Flathub remote configured on your system.

Features

Your whole Immich library

A Photos timeline with a date scrubber, albums, Explore, People, Tags, Folders, Favorites, Archive, and Trash in a native GTK4 window. Rate, archive, and change the date, time zone, or place of many photos at once.

Memories, stacks, and live photos

"On this day" memories above Photos, bursts and RAW+JPEG pairs grouped into stacks, and live and motion photos that play their clip right in the viewer.

A viewer built for big photos

Swipe between photos, zoom to 2000% of the real pixels with the full resolution loading as you go, watch hardware-decoded video streamed from Immich, and run slideshows with crossfade and shuffle. RAW previews included.

Edit without touching the original

Crop, rotate, flip, and straighten; adjust light and colour; apply filters; and redact faces or text. Crops are kept in Immich and can be reverted, and other edits save as a new copy that keeps the original's metadata.

Tag the people in your photos

Name, move, or remove the faces Immich found, draw a box around ones it missed, and point at a face to see who it is.

Find any photo

Smart search, search by the text in a photo (OCR) and copy that text, find similar photos, jump to a photo's place in the timeline, and clear out look-alikes on the Duplicates page.

Your photos on a map

See where your photos were taken, search for any place or address, and pick from Immich's own map style, VersaTiles styles, satellite imagery, or OpenStreetMap.

Share and comment

Shared albums with Viewer and Editor roles, likes and comments, public shared links, and partner sharing, including browsing a partner's photos.

Automatic folder backup

Watch any folder and upload new photos and videos as they appear, to your library, an album, or an album named after the folder. Choose Upload Only, Download Only, or Full sync for each folder; checksums skip files already on your server.

Reliable on any network

Failed uploads are saved and retried, Mimick switches between your home and remote server addresses, and it can pause on metered networks, on battery, or during quiet hours.

Made for the Linux desktop

Tray icon, start on login, drag and drop, Open With Mimick from your file manager, and keyboard shortcuts for nearly everything. The Flatpak only reads folders you pick, and your API key stays in the keyring.

Works on Linux phones

The layout adapts down to 360 pixels wide with a bottom navigation bar, and you can swipe, pinch to zoom, and long-press for menus, so Mimick runs on Linux phones as well as desktops.

Questions

Is there an Immich desktop app for Linux?

Yes. Mimick is a free, open-source Immich desktop client for Linux. It is a community project and not made by the Immich team.

How do I back up a folder to Immich automatically?

Install Mimick from Flathub, enter your server address and an Immich API key, and add the folder under Watch Folders. New photos and videos upload in the background from then on.

Which Linux distributions does it run on?

Any distribution with Flatpak, including Fedora, Ubuntu, Debian, Arch Linux, openSUSE, Linux Mint, and Pop!_OS, on GNOME, KDE Plasma, and other desktops.

Will Mimick change or delete my local files?

Not by default. Folders start as Upload Only, which never changes local files. Download Only and Full sync add your album's photos to the folder.

Which Immich versions are supported?

Immich v3.0.0 and later.
